Most businesses over-think where to start with automation. The best first projects aren't flashy — they're repetitive, well-defined, and low-risk if something goes wrong.

## 1. Lead follow-up

New leads that don't get a response within minutes convert far less often. An automated first-touch message, sent the moment a form is submitted, is one of the highest-ROI automations you can set up.

## 2. Appointment scheduling

Replacing back-and-forth email scheduling with an automated booking flow saves hours a week and reduces no-shows through automated reminders.

## 3. Customer support triage

An AI agent that answers common questions and routes anything complex to a human cuts response time dramatically without removing the human from harder conversations.

## 4. Internal reporting

Pulling numbers from five different tools into one weekly report is exactly the kind of task automation handles well — it's repetitive, rule-based, and easy to verify.

## 5. Data entry between tools

Every manual copy-paste between your CRM, spreadsheet, and invoicing tool is a place where errors creep in. Connecting those tools removes the error and the time cost at once.

## Where to start

Pick the workflow your team complains about most. That's usually the one with the clearest payoff — and the easiest way to prove automation is worth investing in further.
